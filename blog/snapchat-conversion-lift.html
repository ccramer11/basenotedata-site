How Snapchat's Conversion Lift Tests work

How Snapchat's Conversion Lift Test works, what its published examples show, and the outside tools brands use to check Snap's incremental impact.

Chelsea Cramer · Nov 17, 2026

Incrementality testing by platform: Overview · Meta · Google · TikTok · Amazon · Pinterest · Snapchat

Snapchat's Conversion Lift Test compares conversions between people eligible to see your ads and a randomized control group that doesn't, using events from your Snap Pixel. Snap publishes little public documentation on it, so this is a short, cautious summary drawn from case studies and third-party guides.

The idea in one picture

This is the general design. Confirm the specifics with your Snap rep.

What we know

  • Setup: one reporting guide says you start from the Measurement section of Snapchat's Campaign Manager (Keepers Digital).
  • Design: a randomized control experiment that compares conversion rates for exposed and unexposed users.
  • Data: events from the Snap Pixel, with the Conversions API as the server-side route.
  • Metrics: that guide describes absolute lift as the raw increase in conversion rate, in percentage points, caused by your ads.
  • Length: Adore Me ran a month-long test (PPC Land).
  • Multi-cell tests: Home Chef ran multi-cell Brand Lift and Conversion Lift studies to compare strategies.
  • Brand Lift is a separate, survey-based product.

Outside tools

  • Geo holdouts through measurement vendors. One WorkMagic case study found last-click reporting had undercounted Snapchat's performance by up to 63% (WorkMagic). That is a vendor's result for one advertiser.
  • Cross-channel effects. Measured says its analysis of hundreds of brands' incrementality studies found Snapchat also lifts the channels brands already spend the most on (Measured). A Snap-only lift test can't see that halo; a geo test can.
  • Anonym partnered with Snap so brands can measure conversion lift and attribution using their own data privately (Mozilla).
  • InMarket is Snap's preferred measurement partner for offline outcomes such as store visits and sales (MarTech Vibe).

Watch-outs

  • The figures above are headline results from Snap's marketing, with no sample sizes or intervals.
  • Minimum spend, holdout size and required length aren't published. Ask your Snap rep.
  • Snap designs and scores its own test and sees Snap only.
