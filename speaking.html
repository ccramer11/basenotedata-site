Analyst training

Teach your analysts to present data people act on.

Most analysts are great at getting the numbers right and never get taught how to present them. I run hands-on workshops for agency and in-house analytics teams on turning analysis into a clear story for clients, CMOs, and CFOs. I started my career on the agency side and have spent 13+ years presenting to executives, so the material comes from real client meetings and budget reviews.

  • Lead with the answer

    Open with the "so what" and the recommendation, not the methodology, and keep the detail in the appendix for when someone asks.

  • One chart, one point

    Pick the right chart, cut the clutter, and write titles that say what the data means instead of what it shows.

  • Know who's in the room

    Tailor the same analysis for a client, a CMO, and a CFO, and translate marketing metrics into the numbers finance plans around.

  • Handle pushback and uncertainty

    Explain confidence intervals and test results without losing the room, and answer the hard questions with confidence.

Chelsea Cramer is the New York City-based founder of Base Note Data, a marketing measurement consultancy that helps CMOs and CFOs settle the question every budget meeting comes back to: what is marketing actually driving? She works with high-growth brands like Robinhood, Talkiatry, and Stitch Fix on marketing mix modeling, incrementality testing, and causal inference.

Over 13+ years in marketing analytics, she has measured more than $1B in spend. At Credit Karma, she built the TV measurement and MMM programs and unified media measurement as budget grew from $100M to $300M with improving ROI. She was also the first marketing analytics hire at Gympass/Wellhub, where she led the function as Director of Marketing Analytics.
