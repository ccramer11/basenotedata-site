For organizers

Speaker bios.

Short

Chelsea Cramer is the founder of Base Note Data, a marketing measurement consultancy working with high-growth brands like Robinhood, Talkiatry, and Stitch Fix. Before that, she led marketing analytics at Credit Karma and Wellhub, measuring over $1B in spend over her career.

Long

Chelsea Cramer is the founder of Base Note Data, a marketing measurement consultancy that helps CMOs and CFOs settle the question every budget meeting comes back to: what is marketing actually driving? She works with high-growth brands like Robinhood, Talkiatry, and Stitch Fix on marketing mix modeling, incrementality testing, and causal inference.

Over 13+ years in marketing analytics, she has measured more than $1B in spend. At Credit Karma, she built the TV measurement and MMM programs and unified media measurement as budget grew from $100M to $300M with improving ROI. She was also the first marketing analytics hire at Gympass/Wellhub, where she led the function as Director of Marketing Analytics.
