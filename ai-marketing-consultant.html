Where AI helps marketing teams most

I'm Chelsea Cramer, an independent AI marketing consultant with 13+ years in marketing analytics. I help marketing teams build AI workflows and automation into their analytics, so problems get caught the day they happen and analysts spend their time on insight instead of upkeep.

Most marketing analytics teams don't lose time on hard analysis. They lose it on everything around it: pulling the same numbers every Monday, rebuilding the monthly deck, writing up each test plan from scratch, and finding out a data feed broke only when someone notices a strange number in a review.

That work repeats on a schedule and follows clear rules, which is exactly what AI workflows and marketing automation do well. Taking it off your team's plate means they move faster and put their judgment where it matters: what's working, what to change, and where the next dollar should go.

Teams usually bring me in when they want to:

  • Catch performance drops, spend issues and broken data the day they happen, not at the next weekly review
  • Stop spending analyst hours assembling recurring reports
  • Launch tests faster with plans and power analysis that don't start from a blank page
  • Put AI to work on research and sourcing that would otherwise be done by hand
  • Figure out which AI tools and use cases are worth it for their team, and which are hype

How I work

  • Find the time sinks. I look at where your team's hours actually go each week and which tasks repeat.
  • Pick the highest-leverage fix. Usually monitoring or recurring reporting, because they pay back fastest and are easy to check.
  • Build on your stack. Your warehouse, BI tool, Slack and the AI tools you already have. New tools only when they clearly earn it.
  • Check it against the source. Every automated output is validated against the underlying data before anyone relies on it.
  • Hand it off. Your team owns the workflow, knows how it works and can change it.

Because I come from marketing measurement, from marketing mix modeling to incrementality testing, I build these workflows around the numbers that drive budget decisions, not just the ones that are easy to automate.

Common questions

FAQ

What does an AI marketing consultant do?

An AI marketing consultant helps a marketing team decide where AI is worth using and then builds it into their day-to-day work. My focus is the analytics side: monitoring and alerts, automated reporting, testing workflows and research, built so the numbers stay accurate.

Where should a marketing team start with AI in analytics?

Start with work that repeats on a schedule and follows clear rules, like monitoring performance and data feeds, building recurring reports, or drafting test plans. Those are quick to automate, easy to check, and free up the most analyst time.

Will AI replace our analysts?

No. The goal is to take the upkeep off their plate, like pulling data, assembling reports and watching dashboards, so they spend their time on analysis and recommendations.

How do you make sure automated outputs are accurate?

Every workflow is checked against the source data before it goes live, and anything that feeds a budget decision keeps a person reviewing it. Alerts are tuned so the team trusts them instead of tuning them out.

Do we need to buy new tools?

Usually not. I start with what your team already uses, such as your data warehouse, BI tool, Slack and any AI tools you already have, and only suggest something new when it clearly earns its place.
